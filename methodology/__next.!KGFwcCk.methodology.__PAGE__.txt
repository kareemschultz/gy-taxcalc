3:T413,GRA's 2026 notice: GY$140,000, or one-third of Balance of Income after allowing for Second Job and Overtime statutory allowance, whichever is greater. Section 20(1)(a) of the Income Tax Act, as changed by the Fiscal Enactments (Amendment) Act 2026 (Act No. 3 of 2026, which GRA calls the Income Tax (Amendment) Act), raises the fixed amount to GY$1,680,000 a year and keeps the wording of Income Tax (Amendment) Act No. 2 of 2025: "one third of an individual's total income from all sources excluding income subject to withholding taxes under section 39(1)"; it says nothing about taking overtime or second-job allowances off first (they are separate deductions in s.20(1)(c) and (d)). The 2026 Act changed only the fixed amount. The two wordings give the same result unless tax-free overtime or second-job pay is present and pay is near or above GY$420,000 a month; then GRA's reading gives more tax. GYCalc follows GRA's payroll reading: the third is taken of pay less non-taxable allowances and the tax-free overtime and second-job amounts.8:X
